# All of my Irons in the Fire!
*by Mindful Tech Projects*

Life has a lot of irons in the fire: a day job, kids, volunteer work, a honey-do list, a car that needs an oil change, and that food truck you're building so you can retire rich. This plugin helps Claude keep them sorted and keep the ones you care about glowing.

## What it does

- **Greets you and asks what you're working on.** "Hello, Ed. What are we working on today?"
- **Sorts it into the right iron.** Say "food truck" and it knows that's your side project. If it isn't sure, it offers a few guesses.
- **Learns your world.** Every time you confirm or correct, it remembers the words that belong to each iron.
- **Spots new irons.** Start talking about a hang gliding trip and it asks whether that's a new iron.
- **Notices cooling irons.** If something hasn't come up in a while, it checks in gently: heat it up, ask again later, let it rest, or take it out of the fire.
- **Catches up after gaps.** Gone a few days? It asks what's new and what you're done with.
- **Runs a periodic review** (every two weeks, monthly, or never; your choice).
- **Gives an iron its own forge.** When an iron gets big, it can become its own Claude Project, set up for you in your browser if you like.

You're always in charge: nothing is added, archived, or deleted without your yes. Archived irons are kept, not erased.

## Skills

| Skill | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `fire-up` | Start-of-session greeting, sorting, learning, new and cooling irons, first-time setup |
| `irons-review` | Look at everything, tidy up, add, rename, merge, rest, archive, revive |
| `give-it-a-forge` | Turn an iron into its own Claude Project |

## Where your map lives

Your Irons Map is a short, readable note kept in Claude's memory (if memory is on) or in a file called `irons-in-the-fire.md` in your working folder. You can open and edit it anytime.

## Not a security feature

The questions are about direction ("what are we working on?"), not secrets. To keep separate parts of your life truly separate, use separate Claude accounts.
