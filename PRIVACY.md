# Privacy

*All of my Irons in the Fire! · Mindful Tech Projects · last updated 2026-10-26 (version 0.9.1)*

**The plugin doesn't send your information to Mindful Tech Projects.** It has no servers and no analytics.

- **Your irons map** (your irons, keywords, whys, notes, and session snapshots) is stored in your own Claude account's memory, or in a file in your own working folder. Your Claude account is hosted by Anthropic under [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy). Mindful Tech Projects never receives it. You can read, edit, or delete it anytime.
- **You're asked first.** Before saving anything, the plugin tells you what it will remember and asks permission. If you say no, it helps you in the moment without saving. It checks again about every 30 days.
- **What's saved:** your name, your irons and a few keywords for each, why each matters, notes and ideas you ask it to keep, your desk list and done list, your settings, how you describe yourself and your energy level if you choose to share them, and when you last worked on each iron. Only things you tell it or confirm, never whole conversations.
- **How long it's kept:** until you change or erase it.
- **See it or erase it anytime:** say "what do you know about me" to see everything, "forget [something]" to remove one thing, or "forget everything" to erase it all. You'll always be offered an export file first, and "restore my irons" brings it back if you return.
- **Daily nudges** run as a scheduled task in your own Claude account, only if you set one up, delivered by Claude's own phone notification or email (your choice). Texting works only through a service you've connected yourself (for example Zapier, whose texting uses Twilio). A texted or emailed nudge passes through those services and your email provider under their own privacy policies. Nudges never include how you describe yourself, health details, or your energy level, and you can ask for "generic texts" that don't name your irons at all.
- **Update checks** look at this project's public GitHub release page, only when you say yes.
- **Feedback** is something you choose to post, yourself, on this project's public GitHub page. The plugin drafts it with you but never sends it. Don't include anything private; posts there are public.
- **Donations** happen on Patreon, under Patreon's own privacy policy. The plugin never handles payments. If you support the project, Patreon shares your name and email with Mindful Tech Projects; we use them only to thank you and send project updates, and never sell or share them.
- **Adults only.** The plugin is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 in Europe.
- **The website** is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps basic visitor logs under GitHub's privacy statement. It uses no cookies, analytics, or outside fonts.

**Privacy questions or requests:** email privacy@mindfultechsupport.com. Please don't post privacy requests in public GitHub issues.
