# Privacy

*All of my Irons in the Fire! · Mindful Tech Projects · last updated 2026-10-26 (version 0.8.0)*

**The plugin doesn't send your information to Mindful Tech Projects.** It has no servers and no analytics.

- **Your irons map** (your irons, keywords, whys, notes, and session snapshots) is stored in your own Claude account's memory, or in a file in your own working folder. Only you and your Claude can see it. You can read, edit, or delete it anytime.
- **You're asked first.** Before saving anything, the plugin tells you what it will remember and asks permission. If you say no, it helps you in the moment without saving. It checks again about every 90 days.
- **What's saved:** your name, your irons and a few keywords for each, why each matters, notes and ideas you ask it to keep, your desk list, your settings, and when you last worked on each iron. Only things you tell it or confirm, never whole conversations.
- **How long it's kept:** until you change or erase it.
- **See it or erase it anytime:** say "what do you know about me" to see everything, "forget [something]" to remove one thing, or "forget everything" to erase it all (you'll be offered a copy first).
- **Daily nudges** run as a scheduled task in your own Claude account, only if you set one up, delivered by Claude's own phone notification or email (your choice). Texting works only through a service you've connected yourself.
- **Update checks** look at this project's public GitHub release page, only when you say yes.
- **Feedback** is something you choose to post, yourself, on this project's public GitHub page. The plugin drafts it with you but never sends it. Don't include anything private; posts there are public.
- **Donations** happen on Patreon, under Patreon's own privacy policy. The plugin never handles payments.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
