All of my Irons in the Fire!

A free Claude plugin for people with a lot going on. It learns your life, remembers why each iron matters, and picks up right where you left off.

Download the plugin How to install

Current version 0.8.0 · Works in Claude on the web, the desktop app, and Claude Code

ChatBro
Good morning, Ed. Hope you got some rest. Last time we were on the food truck. Want a quick summary?
You
Nah. What's in the fire?
ChatBro
Your irons in the fire:
• Food Truck: 10 hrs
• Day Job: 1 day
• ReservEats: 6 days
• Hang Gliding Trip: 18 days
Ooh, the trip. Let's do that.

Keeps every iron glowing

Knows where you left off

Greets you for the time of day, knows how long you've been away, and offers a quick recap so you're never starting cold.

Remembers your why

When something starts cooling, it reminds you why it mattered to you, not that you're behind. Never guilt.

Catches your sparks

Wander off to another idea mid-task? It helps you get it down, files it where it belongs, and lets you choose what's next.

See it all at a glance

Every iron and how long since you touched it. Sometimes spotting the one you forgot is exactly the energy you need.

Talks when you're driving

Say "I'm driving" and it goes hands-free: short spoken sentences, nothing to look at, screen tasks saved for later.

Your desk list, out loud

Everything that needs a screen lands on one list. Sit down, say "I'm at my desk," and it walks you through 15 minutes of it, one step at a time.

Yours to see or erase

It asks before it remembers anything. "What do you know about me?" shows it all; "forget everything" erases it.

Never throws an iron away

Rest it, put it on hold, or pull it out of the fire. Every iron stays safe and ready for its next turn in the heat.

Install it

You'll need a computer for this step. The Claude phone app can't install a plugin from a file. Once it's on your account, you're set.

Option 1: add the marketplace

  1. In the Claude desktop app or claude.ai, open Customize → Plugins.
  2. Add a marketplace and enter MindfulTechSupport/All-of-the-Irons-in-the-Fire-App-
  3. Install all-my-irons-in-the-fire.

Claude Code: /plugin install all-my-irons-in-the-fire --marketplace MindfulTechSupport/All-of-the-Irons-in-the-Fire-App-

Option 2: install from a file

  1. Download the latest .plugin file.
  2. In Claude, open Customize → Plugins.
  3. Remove an older version if it's listed, then upload the file.

Then start a new chat and say "fire up."
